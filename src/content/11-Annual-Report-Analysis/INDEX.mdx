# 11 - Annual Report Analysis

The four-step checklist institutional analysts use on a 10-K or an Indian annual report: verify the business model and moat, reconcile the three statements, judge management and capital allocation, and set a margin of safety with a DCF and reverse DCF.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 8-10 hours (including one full company)
outcomes:
  - "Verify a company's business model and moat from its own disclosures"
  - "Reconcile earnings with cash flow and spot accounting red flags"
  - "Evaluate management's capital allocation record and insider ownership"
  - "Frame a valuation with a DCF, a reverse DCF and a margin of safety"
prerequisites:
  - "[The Metric Toolkit](../05-Metric-Toolkit/INDEX.mdx)"
  - "[Valuation](../06-Valuation/INDEX.mdx)"
```
