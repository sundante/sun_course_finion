# 02 - Asset Classes & Products

What you can actually own, and what each asset class is for. Cash equivalents, government and corporate bonds, equities, pooled funds (index funds, ETFs and mutual funds) and alternatives (REITs and commodities) each carry a different mix of return, risk, liquidity, cost and tax treatment. Examples cover both US and Indian products.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 5-6 hours
outcomes:
  - "Describe the return drivers, risks, liquidity and tax treatment of each major asset class"
  - "Compare index funds, active ETFs and mutual funds on cost, tax efficiency and tracking error"
  - "Explain duration and credit spread, and estimate a bond's price change for a rate move"
  - "Choose asset classes that fit a stated goal and time horizon"
prerequisites:
  - "[Compound Interest Mechanics](../01-Money-and-Compounding/Notes/01-Compound-Interest-Mechanics.mdx)"
```
