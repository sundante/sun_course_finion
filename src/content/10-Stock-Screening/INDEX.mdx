# 10 - Stock Screening

Screens turn thousands of listed companies into a short list worth researching. This module gives exact numeric and Boolean filters for the growth, dividend and hybrid frameworks on three platforms: Finviz and Koyfin for US stocks, and Screener.in for Indian stocks.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 3-4 hours
outcomes:
  - "Translate a selection framework into precise screener filters"
  - "Run growth, dividend and hybrid screens on Finviz, Koyfin and Screener.in"
  - "Explain the biases screens introduce and why a screen is a starting list, not a buy list"
prerequisites:
  - "[The Metric Toolkit](../05-Metric-Toolkit/INDEX.mdx)"
  - "[GARP + Dividend Growth Framework](../09-GARP-and-Hybrid/Notes/01-GARP-and-Dividend-Growth-Framework.mdx)"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Pick your market: Finviz and Koyfin for US stocks, Screener.in for Indian stocks.
2. Run all three screens (growth, dividend, hybrid) and save them.
3. Take the top five results of one screen into the Annual Report Analysis workflow.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/screening) to check what stuck.
