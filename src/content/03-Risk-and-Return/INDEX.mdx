# 03 - Risk & Return

Return means nothing without the risk taken to earn it. This module measures both: arithmetic and geometric returns, volatility, and the two risk-adjusted ratios every fund factsheet quotes - Sharpe and Sortino - plus drawdown and beta.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 3-4 hours
outcomes:
  - "Calculate and annualize the Sharpe and Sortino ratios from monthly returns"
  - "Explain when Sortino is more informative than Sharpe"
  - "Interpret risk-adjusted numbers on a fund factsheet and spot when they mislead"
prerequisites:
  - "[Compound Interest Mechanics](../01-Money-and-Compounding/Notes/01-Compound-Interest-Mechanics.mdx)"
```
