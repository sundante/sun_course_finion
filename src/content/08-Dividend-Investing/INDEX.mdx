# 08 - Dividend Growth Investing

Dividend growth investing looks for companies that raise their payout every year, faster than inflation, from cash they can afford. This module covers the Aristocrat and Contender lists, a dividend safety checklist, and the balance sheet and capital intensity that let a dividend survive a recession.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 4-5 hours
outcomes:
  - "Explain the Dividend Aristocrat, Contender and King categories and their limits"
  - "Assess dividend safety with FCF payout, coverage, leverage and dividend growth vs inflation"
  - "Distinguish a sustainable high yield from a yield trap"
prerequisites:
  - "[Dividend Payout & FCF Coverage](../05-Metric-Toolkit/Notes/07-Dividend-Payout-and-FCF-Coverage.mdx)"
```
