# 15 - Licensing & Career

The credentials that turn knowledge into a profession. This module maps the US path (SIE, Series 7, 63, 65 and 66, CFP), the global CFA Program, and the Indian path (NISM certifications and SEBI registration as an investment adviser or research analyst), with study checklists for each.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 3 hours to plan; months to years to complete
outcomes:
  - "Choose the licenses and designations that fit the advisory role you want, in the US or India"
  - "Explain what each exam covers, who can sit it, and what it permits"
  - "Build a study plan from the checklists"
prerequisites:
  - "[Fiduciary Duty](../14-Advisory-Practice/Notes/01-Fiduciary-Duty.mdx)"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Read the path for your country, then the CFA Program.
2. Pick one credential and copy its checklist into your repository.

> Exam formats, fees and eligibility rules change. Treat these notes as a map and confirm details with FINRA, NASAA, CFP Board, CFA Institute, NISM and SEBI before registering.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/career) to check what stuck.
