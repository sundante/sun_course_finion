# 12 - Research Templates

Templates turn analysis into a repeatable habit. This module sets up a research repository (Obsidian or GitHub) and provides three downloadable templates: the company deep dive, the quarterly earnings note, and the investment thesis with its post-mortem.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 2 hours to set up, then ongoing
outcomes:
  - "Set up a research repository with a clear folder taxonomy"
  - "Write a company deep dive, a quarterly earnings note and an investment thesis from the templates"
  - "Run a post-mortem that separates bad luck from bad analysis"
prerequisites:
  - "[Annual Report Analysis](../11-Annual-Report-Analysis/INDEX.mdx)"
```
