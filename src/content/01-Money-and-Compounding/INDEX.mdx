# 01 - Money & Compounding

How money grows, and what quietly stops it growing. This module builds the compounding formula from first principles, shows why the rate you reinvest at matters as much as the rate you earn, and measures the drag that fees, taxes and inflation put on terminal wealth. Everything later in the course - valuation, dividends, portfolio design - rests on these mechanics.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 4-5 hours
outcomes:
  - "Calculate future value with any compounding frequency, including continuous compounding and regular contributions"
  - "Explain reinvestment risk and why a bond's promised yield is not the return you will earn"
  - "Quantify how much of a portfolio's terminal wealth is lost to fees, taxes and inflation over 10-40 years"
  - "Use the Rule of 72 to estimate doubling times in your head"
prerequisites:
  - "None - this is the starting point of the course"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Read Compound Interest Mechanics and do its exercises with a spreadsheet open.
2. Read Reinvestment Risk, then Terminal Wealth Drag - the second builds on the first.
3. Rebuild the fee-drag table from Terminal Wealth Drag for your own savings rate and fund costs.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/compounding) to check what stuck.
