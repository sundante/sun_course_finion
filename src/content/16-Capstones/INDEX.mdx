# 16 - Capstones

Three end-to-end projects that prove the skills from the whole course: a full equity research report, a model portfolio with an investment policy statement, and a client advisory case study.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 15-25 hours
outcomes:
  - "Produce an institutional-quality equity research report on one listed company"
  - "Build and document a model portfolio with an allocation, rebalancing rules and risk metrics"
  - "Deliver a written, suitability-compliant recommendation for a fictional client"
prerequisites:
  - "All previous modules"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Do the equity research report first - it uses the most modules.
2. Publish your capstones in your research repository so you can show them to employers.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/capstones) to check what stuck.
