# 14 - Advisory Practice

Advising other people is a different job from picking stocks for yourself. This module covers the fiduciary standard, client risk profiling, matching assets to a client's liabilities, and tax-efficient withdrawal strategies in the US and India.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 5-6 hours
outcomes:
  - "Explain the fiduciary standard and how it differs from suitability and Regulation Best Interest"
  - "Profile a client's risk tolerance, risk capacity and risk need"
  - "Design a withdrawal order that reduces lifetime tax in the US and in India"
prerequisites:
  - "[Modern Portfolio Theory](../13-Portfolio-Construction/Notes/01-Modern-Portfolio-Theory.mdx)"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Read Fiduciary Duty first - it frames every other decision.
2. Run the risk profiling questionnaire on yourself, then on a friend.

> Tax rules change often. Every figure in this module is dated; confirm current limits with the IRS or the Income Tax Department before using them.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/advisory) to check what stuck.
