# 04 - Accounting Foundations

Financial statements are the language every metric in this course is written in. This module covers the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement, and how the three connect - under both US GAAP and Ind AS.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 5-6 hours
outcomes:
  - "Read an income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement and name their key lines"
  - "Trace one transaction through all three statements"
  - "Reconcile net income to cash from operations"
prerequisites:
  - "None"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. This module is being written. Until it is complete, the SEC's Beginners' Guide to Financial Statements is a good free primer.
2. Download one 10-K and one Indian annual report and find each statement in them.

> The Metric Toolkit assumes you can find EBIT, total debt, equity and cash from operations in a filing. If you cannot yet, start here.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/accounting) to check what stuck.
