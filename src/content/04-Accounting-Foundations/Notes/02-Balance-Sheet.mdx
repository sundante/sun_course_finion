# Balance Sheet

The balance sheet is a snapshot of what a company owns (assets), owes (liabilities) and what is left for shareholders (equity) on one date.

This page is being written. Planned coverage:

- Assets = Liabilities + Equity
- Working capital: receivables, inventory, payables
- Goodwill, intangibles and impairment
- Leases, provisions and off-balance-sheet items
