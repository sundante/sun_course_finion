# Income Statement

The income statement reports revenue, costs and profit over a period - the accrual-basis story of how a company earned its money.

This page is being written. Planned coverage:

- Revenue recognition basics (ASC 606 / Ind AS 115)
- Gross profit, EBITDA, EBIT, pre-tax and net income
- One-offs, exceptional items and adjusted earnings
- Diluted EPS and share count
