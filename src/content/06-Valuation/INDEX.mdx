# 06 - Valuation

What is a business worth, and what is the market already assuming? This module builds a discounted cash flow model, then runs it in reverse to extract the growth the current price implies, and ends with the margin of safety that protects you when your estimates are wrong.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 6-8 hours
outcomes:
  - "Build a three-stage DCF from free cash flow forecasts, WACC and a terminal value"
  - "Run a reverse DCF to find the growth rate the market price implies"
  - "Set a margin of safety that reflects the uncertainty of a business"
  - "Explain why terminal value dominates most DCFs, and how to sanity-check it"
prerequisites:
  - "[ROIC vs WACC](../05-Metric-Toolkit/Notes/03-ROIC-vs-WACC.mdx)"
  - "[Free Cash Flow Yield](../05-Metric-Toolkit/Notes/04-FCF-Yield.mdx)"
```
