# 07 - Growth Investing

Growth investing pays for the future, so the analysis has to be about the future: how big the market can get, whether the unit economics work, and what warns you before the multiple collapses. This module is the high-growth selection framework.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 5-6 hours
outcomes:
  - "Size a market top-down and bottom-up with TAM, SAM and SOM"
  - "Evaluate unit economics with CAC, LTV, payback and gross margin"
  - "Apply the Rule of 40 to software companies and explain its limits"
  - "List the early warning signs of multiple compression"
prerequisites:
  - "[P/E vs PEG](../05-Metric-Toolkit/Notes/01-PE-vs-PEG.mdx)"
  - "[Margin Trajectory](../05-Metric-Toolkit/Notes/06-Margin-Trajectory.mdx)"
```

## How to Study This Module

1. Read the four notes in order - they follow the shape of a growth thesis.
2. Run the growth screen from Finviz Recipes or Screener.in Recipes, and test three results against these notes.

When you finish, take the [module quiz](/quiz/growth) to check what stuck.
